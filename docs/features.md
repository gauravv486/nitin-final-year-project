# CA-OS Project - Feature List

## 1. Practice Operating System
- **Dashboard & Navigation**: Comprehensive firm-wide KPIs, active timelines, and alerts.
- **AI Client Onboarding**: PAN/GSTIN registration lookup wizard and automated engagement letter builder.
- **Document Management**: Smart folder layouts, drag-and-drop secure upload portal, and OCR ingestion.
- **Bookkeeping Automation**: Auto ledger mappings with confidence scores, ledger summaries, and expense categorizations.
- **Client Communication**: Contextual chat threads, client portal uploads, approval trackers, and WhatsApp follow-up configurations.

## 2. Compliance Operating System
- **GST & Reconciliation**: GSTR-2B reconciliation view, vendor mismatch logs, and ITC risk scores.
- **Income Tax & AIS**: AIS/TIS comparison against book entries and tax planning advice.
- **Notices & Litigation**: Notice extraction, case law semantic searches, and response drafts.
- **Payroll & Statutory Compliance**: Automatic PF/ESI calculations and Payslips.
- **Corporate Secretarial (MCA/ROC)**: ROC AGM notices, resolution generators, and e-form SRN lists.
- **Compliance Calendar**: 30-day deadlines calendar with automatic reminders.

## 3. AI Intelligence Features
- **CA Intelligence Engine**: Powered by LangGraph agents for OCR extraction, RAG case law matching, ledger mapping, and conversational querying.
- **Risk Scoring & 100% Audit**: AI-powered risk assessment on all transactions, identifying high-risk and suspicious entries.
- **Financial Insights & AI Recommendations**: Actionable insights on revenue growth, unusual expense patterns, and tax-saving opportunities (e.g., Section 80C).

## 4. Security Operating System
- **Security Console**: Granular RBAC, DPDP-compliant consent checklists, and immutable audit logs.

## 5. Experience Operating System
- **Settings Center**: White-label firm layout preview, custom notification routes, and mobile-responsive warnings.
- **Reports & Export Hub**: Interactive drill-downs, whitelist customizations, password lock controls, digital signature certificates, and scheduled dispatches.
