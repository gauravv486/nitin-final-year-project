## Overview

**CA·OS** has been completely redesigned with a professional **Government Chartered Accountant (CA) theme** featuring a clean, minimalist, and user-friendly interface inspired by official government portals.

## Design System

### Color Palette
- **Primary Navy**: `#1B3A6B` — Trust, Authority, Government Official
- **Primary Dark**: `#0F2849` — Deep authority for emphasis
- **Gold Accent**: `#D4AF37` — Prestige and excellence
- **Light Gold**: `#F5E6CC` — Subtle highlights for active states
- **Status Colors**:
  - Success Green: `#27AE60` with light background `#D5F4E6`
  - Warning Amber: `#E8A53E` with light background `#FDF5E8`
  - Danger Red: `#C0392B` with light background `#FADBD8`
  - Info Blue: `#2980B9` with light background `#D6EAF8`

### Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter (system default)
- **Headings**: 28px (h1) with navy primary color, 700 weight
- **Body Text**: 13.5px gray-600 for readability
- **Labels**: 10.5-11.5px uppercase with proper letter spacing

### Layout & Spacing
- **Sidebar Width**: 260px with white background and right border
- **Header Height**: 65px with subtle shadow
- **Card Padding**: 24px with proper hierarchy
- **Border Radius**: 8px (standard), 12px (large)
- **Shadow**: Minimal, professional shadow for depth
